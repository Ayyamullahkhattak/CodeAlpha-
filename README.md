# ❤️ Cardiovascular Health Analysis — EDA & Data Visualization

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and Data Visualization** of a cardiovascular health dataset containing approximately 400,000 records. The objective is to explore patient demographics, analyze cardiovascular risk factors, and compare health indicators between youth and adults using Python.

This project was completed as part of my **CodeAlpha Data Analytics Internship**.


## 📂 About the Dataset

The dataset contains approximately **400,000 records** related to cardiovascular health, demographic characteristics, and potential risk factors. It includes variables such as age, gender, smoking, alcohol consumption, diabetes, hypertension, blood sugar, blood pressure, cholesterol levels, chest pain, and cardiac arrest.

The dataset was used to perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** and **Data Visualization**, comparing cardiovascular health indicators and risk factors between youth and adults.

**Dataset Source:** [Click here to access the dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ankushpanday1/heart-attack-risk-in-youth-vs-adult-in-pakistan)

## 📊 Task 1: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

The EDA task involved inspecting, cleaning, and analyzing the dataset to understand its structure, demographic distributions, and cardiovascular health indicators.

### 🔍 EDA Questions

1. What is the age and gender distribution of records in the dataset?
2. How many records belong to the youth and adult groups?
3. Which cardiovascular risk factors are most common?
4. How do cardiovascular risk factors differ between youth and adults?
5. Is smoking more prevalent among youth or adults?
6. How do blood pressure and cholesterol levels differ between the two age groups?
7. Does the distribution of chest pain differ between youth and adults?

### 📈 Key Findings

* **Gender Distribution:** Approximately 50.07% females and 49.93% males.
* **Age Groups:** 97,215 youth records and 302,779 adult records under the project's age-group classification.
* **Blood Sugar Risk:** High blood sugar was observed in 57.10% of youth and 57.13% of adults.
* **Common Risk Factors:** High blood sugar, smoking, alcohol consumption, diabetes, drug use, and hypertension were examined.
* **Clinical Indicators:** Blood pressure, cholesterol levels, and chest pain patterns were compared between youth and adults.

## ⚠️ Important Note

The dataset includes a `Cardiac_Arrest` variable, but **cardiac arrest and heart attack are not the same medical condition**. Therefore, the analysis focuses on cardiovascular health indicators and risk factors rather than assuming that every record represents a confirmed heart-attack patient.

The findings describe patterns in the available dataset and do not establish causation or medical diagnoses.
